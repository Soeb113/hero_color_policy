Privacy Policy for Color Hero

"Look • Pick • Brush • Paint!"
Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to Color Hero ("we", "our", or "the App"), a child-friendly color matching and figure painting puzzle game for Android. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you download, install, and play Color Hero.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Color Hero does not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Safe for Kids: Color Hero is designed as a family-friendly game suitable for children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-Kids), and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

In accordance with child privacy laws:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).
  • No user accounts, profiles, social logins, or chat features exist within the game.
  • No user-created artwork or gameplay progress leaves the local device.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove it.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Color Hero operates locally on your Android device. All core game progress and custom preferences are stored solely on your device using standard Android Local Storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Level Progress & Stars: Unlocked levels, stars earned (1–3 stars), and highest scores.
  • In-game Currencies: Saved in-game coins (🪙) and hints (💡) earned by completing levels.
  • Hero Collection: Characters unlocked by beating levels.
  • "My Art" Gallery: Color completion history displayed in the in-app art album.
  • Settings: Sound and audio effects toggles.

This data remains strictly on your device. You can reset all game data at any time via Settings → Reset Progress or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.

4. Device Permissions

Color Hero requests only minimal, standard technical permissions required to deliver game functionality and load optional advertisements:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables network access to load advertisement banners and video rewards.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to verify whether an active internet connection is available before attempting network requests.

We do not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Read/Write External Storage, Contacts, SMS, Phone State, or Location.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Color Hero is a free game supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable technical information in order to deliver and measure ads.

Ad Formats Implemented:

  • Bottom Banner Ads: Displayed on non-gameplay screens (Home, World Map, Hero Collection, and My Art Album). Never displayed during active painting to prevent accidental taps.
  • Interstitial Ads: Shown only during natural game breaks (after completing a level) with strict frequency limits (once every 3–4 completed levels with a cooldown).
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Displayed strictly upon voluntary request by the player (e.g., opting in to receive bonus in-game coins). Never forced.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's policies:

Google Advertising Privacy & Terms

6. Offline Playability

An internet connection is not required to enjoy Color Hero. All painting mechanics, 100 puzzle levels, hero unlocks, tools, brush effects, sounds, and art albums work completely offline. When offline, advertisements simply remain unavailable without impacting gameplay.

7. Data Security

Because Color Hero does not operate an external database or transmit user personal data to remote servers, there is no personal data exposed to server-side breaches. Local game preferences are secured by Android's application sandbox security model.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example, to reflect updates in legal requirements or platform policies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
